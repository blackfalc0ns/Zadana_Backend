# Zadana Driver App Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

**Zadana** respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use the Driver App and related platform services in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

By using the app, creating an account, or accepting delivery tasks, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

This policy complements the Zadana Driver App Terms and Conditions.

## 1. Who we are

Zadana is a multi-sided marketplace platform connecting customers, merchants, and delivery drivers. We provide the technology that coordinates ordering, payment, tracking, support, and driver payout settlement.

For privacy inquiries:

- Email: [support@zadna0.com](mailto:support@zadna0.com)
- Or through official support channels inside the Zadana Driver App

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect and process:

1. **Account and contact data:** such as name, mobile number and/or email, and one-time password (OTP) verification data.
2. **Identity and verification data:** verification information and documents required to activate the account, such as national ID or residency, driving license, personal photo, and any other documents required by the Platform.
3. **Vehicle data:** vehicle type or category, registration/license details, and other information needed for operational account approval.
4. **Location data:** your geographic location when your status is “Available” and while fulfilling an active delivery task, to assign orders and track delivery.
5. **Operations and order data:** offer accept/reject history, task status, tracking events, delivery codes when needed, and notes about failed delivery or operational issues.
6. **Financial and operational data:** delivery fees, Wallet balances, pending amounts, cash-on-delivery amounts, withdrawal requests, and bank payout details as needed for settlement.
7. **Device and notification data:** device identifiers or push tokens needed to send delivery offers and important alerts.
8. **Usage and support data:** limited technical logs and support interactions needed to resolve issues and prevent fraud or abuse.

## 3. Purposes of processing

We use your data to:

1. Create and manage your account and verify your identity and documents when needed.
2. Determine eligibility to receive orders and approve your operating scope (city/area).
3. Dispatch delivery offers, track task fulfillment, and improve service quality.
4. Share the minimum necessary tracking data with the customer during an active task.
5. Calculate earnings, settle payments, and manage the Wallet and withdrawal requests.
6. Manage cash-on-delivery amounts and operational settlement follow-up.
7. Send notifications related to orders, account status, documents, and the Wallet.
8. Provide support and handle operational or financial complaints and disputes.
9. Protect the platform from fraud and abuse and comply with legal obligations.
10. Improve reliability, safety, and dispatch quality within what is needed for operations.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We may share the minimum necessary data with:

1. **The customer** during an active delivery task, such as display name, delivery status, and location as needed for tracking.
2. **The merchant** to the operational extent needed to collect and hand over the order.
3. **Payment or payout providers** to process settlements and withdrawals when applicable.
4. **Technical service providers** (such as hosting, messaging/OTP, notifications, media, maps) as needed to operate the platform.
5. **Competent authorities** when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Location and notifications

1. Your location is used to assign orders and track delivery while you are available for work or fulfilling an active task.
2. Your location may be shared with the customer only during an active task, not as continuous tracking outside that context.
3. We may send notifications related to delivery offers, account status, documents, and the Wallet through the app or available channels.
4. Keeping notifications and location enabled while working helps you receive offers and complete tasks correctly.

## 6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud. When no longer needed, we delete or de-identify the data according to operating practices, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

We may retain operational and financial records after account closure for as long as needed to meet legal obligations, settle disputes, and protect rights.

## 7. Your rights

Under applicable laws in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, you may have rights to access, correct, request deletion of, or restrict processing of your data, within the limits permitted by law and service requirements. You can contact us at [support@zadna0.com](mailto:support@zadna0.com) or through the app, and we will handle your request within a reasonable time.

Some data may not be deleted immediately if it is still needed for an outstanding financial settlement, dispute, or legal obligation.

## 8. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for not allowing others to use your account.

## 9. Eligibility

The Driver App is intended for people who have the legal capacity to provide delivery services in the Kingdom and who meet applicable verification and licensing requirements. If we learn that data was collected in violation of this, we will take appropriate steps, including account suspension or deletion where possible.

## 10. Third-party links and services

The app may integrate with third-party services (such as maps or payment/payout providers). Those parties’ practices are governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices outside our operational integration.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, laws, or operating practices. The updated version takes effect when published in the app or on an announced date. For material changes, we may ask you to review or accept the update before continuing use.

## 12. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including applicable personal data protection laws. If amicable resolution through support channels is not possible, the competent authorities in the Kingdom shall have jurisdiction unless otherwise required by law.
