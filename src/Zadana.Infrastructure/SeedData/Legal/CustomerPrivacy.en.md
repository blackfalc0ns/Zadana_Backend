**Zadana** respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use the Customer App and related platform services in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

By using the app, creating an account, or placing an order, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

This policy complements the Customer App Terms of Use.

## 1. Who we are

Zadana is a multi-sided marketplace platform connecting customers, vendors, and delivery drivers. We provide the technology that coordinates ordering, payment, tracking, and support.

For privacy inquiries:

- Email: [support@zadna0.com](mailto:support@zadna0.com)
- Or through support channels inside the Zadana Customer App

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect and process:

1. **Account data:** such as mobile number and/or email, display name if provided, and one-time password (OTP) verification data.
2. **Addresses and location:** delivery addresses, coordinates, and area needed to assess delivery availability and fees.
3. **Order data:** cart contents, order details, order status, complaints, or return requests.
4. **Payment data:** payment status, payment method, and identifiers needed for verification. Card payments are processed by an approved payment provider (currently Moyasar); full card details are not sent directly to Zadana servers, and we do not store payment secrets in the app.
5. **Device and notification data:** device identifiers or push tokens needed to send order status alerts.
6. **Usage and support data:** limited technical logs and support interactions needed to resolve issues and prevent abuse.
7. **Driver location (display only):** the driver’s location may be shown to you only in the context of an active assignment linked to your order.

## 3. Purposes of processing

We use your data to:

1. Create and manage your account and verify your identity when needed.
2. Enable product browsing, order creation, and delivery fulfillment.
3. Calculate fees, taxes, available payment methods, and show the order summary.
4. Process payments and confirm payment status through the payment provider.
5. Track order status and send related notifications.
6. Provide support and handle complaints and returns under platform policies.
7. Protect the platform from fraud and abuse and comply with legal obligations.
8. Improve service reliability within what is needed for operations.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We may share the minimum necessary data with:

1. **The vendor** to prepare and fulfill your order.
2. **The driver** to complete delivery and operational communication related to the order.
3. **The payment provider** to process electronic payments and confirm their status.
4. **Technical service providers** (such as hosting, messaging/OTP, notifications, media) as needed to operate the platform.
5. **Competent authorities** when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Location and notifications

1. We use your delivery address and coordinates to enable the service and calculate fees and delivery availability.
2. We may send notifications related to your account or orders through the app or available channels.
3. The driver’s location is shown to you only during an active assignment linked to your order, not as continuous tracking outside that context.

## 6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud. When no longer needed, we delete or de-identify the data according to operating practices, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 7. Your rights

Under applicable laws in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, you may have rights to access, correct, request deletion of, or restrict processing of your data, within the limits permitted by law and service requirements. You can contact us at [support@zadna0.com](mailto:support@zadna0.com) or through the app, and we will handle your request within a reasonable time.

## 8. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

## 9. Children

Zadana services are intended for people who can legally contract and use ordering and delivery services in the Kingdom. We do not knowingly target those who lack legal capacity. If we learn that data was collected in violation of this, we will take appropriate steps, including deletion where possible.

## 10. Third-party links and services

The app may integrate with third-party services (such as a payment provider). Those parties’ practices are governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices outside our operational integration.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, laws, or operating practices. The updated version takes effect when published on the website or in the app, or on an announced date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

## 12. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including applicable personal data protection laws. If amicable resolution through support channels is not possible, the competent authorities in the Kingdom shall have jurisdiction unless otherwise required by law.
